# Chess Game

I made a two player chess game that you and your firend  can play on the same screen. 
It looks pretty nice.

## theme

Co op (glowstone)
because this game can be playable by two people 

only
you and your friend needs to involve in this to play this gamee


## Run

just click on start 
and then click on pawns
and then select the path 

## Play

1. The white player goes first.

2. Click on one of the white player’s pieces to start.

3. You will see some squares and those are the places you can move to.

4. Click on a square to move or capture a piece.

5. Then it will be the player’s turn.

6. If you want to start again you can click New Game to reset the board.

## cool feature

On the side you can see:

- All the pieces that the white player captured

- All the pieces that the black player captured


## movement

- The pawns can move forward one or two spaces...

- The pawns can capture diagonally...

- The rook, bishop, queen knight and king can all move around....

- The king can castle with the rook but only if they have not moved and there are no pieces in the way
